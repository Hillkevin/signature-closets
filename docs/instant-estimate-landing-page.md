## Section: How the Estimate Works (H2)

**H2:** How We Calculate Your Estimate

Short, transparent explanation — this builds trust and gives Google real text content to index around the tool:

- **Size:** We calculate based on the width and height you enter
- **Material:** Melamine, laminate, and wood veneer each have different price-per-square-foot rates
- **Add-ons:** Features like lighting, soft-close drawers, and islands add a flat amount to your estimate
- **Range, not exact price:** Because every room has quirks — outlets, vents, uneven walls — we show a range. Your final number is locked in once we've measured your actual space, for free.
